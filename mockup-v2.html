Chapter Three

The Beginning of Knowledge

❦ ❦ ❦

The Reverend Mother Gaius Helen Mohiam came down from her chambers, walking slowly through the cool stone corridors of Castle Caladan, where centuries of Atreides ancestors had walked before her. Far below, the waves moved with the unhurried patience of something that knew it would outlast every kingdom.

Paul lay awake in his chamber. He had felt the test before it began — not as memory, but as something older, a knowing that came from somewhere beneath thought. His mother had warned him, in her oblique Bene Gesserit way, that the day would come when he would be measured against an ancient standard.

He closed his hand into a fist, then opened it. The chamber smelled faintly of melange — the spice that drifted through every corridor of Caladan now that the Imperial decree had been read aloud in the great hall.
